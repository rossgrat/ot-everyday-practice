# Practice sessions

Choose a practice set and whether hints are available before starting.
Three sets vary dates, amounts, and balances. The task's displayed date is the scenario date.
Set B requires a savings-to-checking transfer before scheduling payment.

The exercises ask the learner to:

1. Read the phone bill's due date.
2. Check whether the available checking balance covers the bill.
3. Schedule full payment by the due date and open its confirmation, without extra active payments.
4. Read an existing confirmation's amount, date, and confirmation number.
5. Identify and cancel a seeded incorrect payment.

Switch between Harbor Bank and Cedar Mobile throughout the exercise.
Payments appear in both services.
Scheduled payments do not settle or debit balances; transfers change available balances.
The simulation does not model future income.

## Assistance and results

Use Therapist tools to record assistance, review records, add observations, restart, or end a session.
Requested hints, unsuccessful checks, submitted payment mistakes, and later corrections remain in the event history.
Missing assistance records carry `Assistance not reviewed`.
Completion alone does not establish independence, a diagnosis, or a standardized clinical score.

`Check task` completes a successful session or lists outstanding criteria while allowing more practice.
End an incomplete session through Therapist tools to preserve its results.
Download a text report, save a confirmation, or print a summary.
Keep personal details out of observations.

## Timing and storage

Pause explicitly for breaks. Paused time does not count toward active time.
Returning to the exercise library also pauses the session.
After reload, time away counts unless the therapist paused first.

The browser retains the active session and up to 50 ended sessions locally.
Clearing site data deletes those records.
Practice can continue after a storage failure; download results before leaving.
There are no patient accounts, external requests, analytics, cloud storage, or real financial connections.
